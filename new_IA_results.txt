V1, 200 000 games
The AI learned from links between normal cards. At the end of each game, it checked which cards helped other cards score. 
For example, if a condition card was completed, the AI rewarded the condition card and the resource cards that helped complete it. 
If a card scored points from night, parchment, colors, or resources, the AI rewarded both the scoring card and the useful cards that made those points possible.
